Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 9, 2026

Overview

Accord ("we," "us," or "our") is a multi-model AI chat app for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app processes, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information We Process

  • Messages and prompts you type or dictate in the app
  • Images you attach or capture for vision-capable models
  • Voice input converted to text on your device or by Apple speech services
  • Chat history stored locally on your device
  • Subscription status from Apple (we do not receive your Apple ID password)
  • Optional Face ID / Touch ID usage to unlock the app (processed by iOS; we do not receive biometric data)
  • Basic app diagnostics when you choose to copy diagnostics in advanced builds

How Your Data Is Used

  • To send your prompts to the AI model you select and return responses
  • To save, encrypt, and display your chat history on your device
  • To enforce subscription tiers and daily usage limits
  • To provide voice input, camera, and photo attachment features you request
  • To improve reliability and support when you contact us

Third-Party AI Providers

When you use cloud models, your prompts and any attached images may be transmitted to third-party providers (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, and xAI) to generate responses. Each provider processes data under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not sell your personal information.

Do not submit sensitive personal, medical, legal, or financial information you would not want processed by an AI service.

Storage & Security

Saved chats are encrypted on your device before being written to local storage. The encryption key is stored in the iOS Keychain. Draft chats are not saved until you send a message.

We do not operate a user account system. Your chat history stays on your device unless you export or share it.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete individual chats from history or delete all saved chats in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes local app data from your device.

Subscription records are managed by Apple. Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Children

Accord is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
