Getting Started

Tap the model name in the chat header to switch AI models. Type a message or use the microphone for voice input. Attach images with the + button when using vision-capable models.

Subscriptions & Usage

View or change your plan in Settings → Subscription. Daily token usage is shown in the chat screen and Settings. Free plans include 2 models per brand and a daily token allowance; paid plans unlock additional models and higher limits.

Use Restore Purchases if you reinstall the app or switch devices.

Privacy & Security

Saved chats are encrypted on your device. Enable Face ID Lock in Settings for extra protection.

Read our Privacy Policy for full details on data handling and third-party AI providers.

Troubleshooting

  • No response: check your internet connection and try again
  • Model unavailable: try another model or check your subscription tier
  • Daily limit reached: wait until midnight local time or upgrade your plan
  • Voice not working: allow Microphone and Speech Recognition in iOS Settings → Accord
  • Photos/camera: allow permissions in iOS Settings → Accord

Contact Support

Email us at feedback@accord.chat. Include your app version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and a description of the issue.
